About us

Apellas story

From a nails and barber shop to a full beauty lounge

Apellas began as a small nails and barber shop in Ugunja town. Clients kept coming back and asking for more, so it grew into Apellas Beauty Lounge: nails, hair, barbering, facials, make-up and lashes under one roof.

Today the lounge is run by owner Owade Quinter, and the idea is simple. Every client should leave looking sharp and feeling confident.

Look Good. Feel Good. Be You.

Our commitment to beauty

We listen first, then work carefully on every cut, set of nails and treatment, so the result matches what you asked for.

Our customer experience

Walk into a bright, calm lounge, sit at our mirror stations and relax in the lounge corner while we work.

Why choose us

One lounge for nails, hair, barbering and spa care, clear prices in KES, easy WhatsApp booking and house calls on request.
